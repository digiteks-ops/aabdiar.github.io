Request Account and Data Deletion

MATCHPOIN respects your privacy and provides users with the right to request deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to our support email address.
  2. Use the subject: MATCHPOIN Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your MATCHPOIN account.
  4. Our team will verify your account ownership before processing the request.

What Happens After Your Request?

After verification, MATCHPOIN will process your deletion request, including:

  • Deleting your account and personal profile information.
  • Removing associated authentication information.
  • Deleting or anonymizing associated personal data where applicable.

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Facebook Login Users

If you used Facebook Login, you can request deletion using the same procedure.

Removing MATCHPOIN from your Facebook account does not automatically delete your MATCHPOIN account.
