Welcome to MATCHPOIN. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

We may collect information including:

When you sign in using Facebook, MATCHPOIN may receive information authorized by you, such as your Facebook user ID, name, email address, and profile picture.

MATCHPOIN uses Supabase infrastructure for authentication and database services. We take reasonable measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or modification.

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate MATCHPOIN or when required by law.

Some player profile information, match results, and ratings may be visible to other users according to platform features and privacy settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information. To request account deletion, visit our Data Deletion page.

8. Children's Privacy

MATCHPOIN is not intended for children under 13 without appropriate parental authorization. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate authorization.
